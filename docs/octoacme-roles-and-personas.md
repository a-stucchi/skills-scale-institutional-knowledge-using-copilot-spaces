# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers are responsible for shaping the user experience by creating user flows, wireframes, and prototypes. They bridge the gap between user needs and technical implementation, ensuring that products are intuitive, accessible, and aligned with user expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Design user flows, wireframes, and interactive prototypes
- Conduct user research and usability testing to validate designs
- Collaborate with Product Managers to align on user problems and solutions
- Hand off design specifications to Developers for implementation
- Work with QA to ensure designs are testable and acceptance criteria are met
- Maintain and evolve the design system and component library

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and high-quality user experiences
- Reduce usability issues identified in QA and post-release feedback
- Ensure design decisions are grounded in user research and data

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critiques with Product and Engineering
- Handoff documentation (specs, assets, and annotations)
- Usability test findings shared with the broader team

---

## Data Analyst

### Role Summary
Data Analysts support projects by providing analytics, defining success metrics, and monitoring the impact of releases. They enable data-driven decision-making across the team by translating raw data into actionable insights.

### Responsibilities
- Define and document success metrics in collaboration with the Product Lead
- Build and maintain dashboards for project and product KPIs
- Analyze usage data and post-release impact to inform iteration
- Work with Developers to ensure proper instrumentation and data collection
- Provide regular reporting to Project Managers for stakeholder updates
- Identify trends, anomalies, and opportunities in project data

### Goals
- Enable data-informed prioritization and decision-making
- Ensure success metrics are measurable and tracked throughout the project lifecycle
- Surface insights that help the team iterate and improve outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Dashboard and report sharing with Product and Project Managers
- Metric definition sessions during project initiation and planning
- Post-release analysis summaries shared with stakeholders

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Facilitator

### Role Summary
The Scrum Master or Agile Facilitator enables the delivery team to work effectively by facilitating agile ceremonies, removing blockers, and fostering a culture of continuous improvement. They act as a servant-leader, supporting the team rather than directing it.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, sprint reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove blockers that impede team progress
- Coach the team on agile principles and practices
- Collaborate with the Project Manager on delivery cadence and milestone tracking
- Support the Product Lead in backlog refinement and prioritization sessions
- Track and report on team velocity, capacity, and sprint health

### Goals
- Enable a high-performing, self-organizing delivery team
- Reduce blockers and improve team flow and throughput
- Drive continuous improvement through effective retrospectives

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies with the full delivery team
- Blocker escalations to Project Manager and Product Lead
- Retrospective action items tracked and reviewed in weekly PM syncs

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
The QA Lead owns the testing strategy and quality assurance process for projects. They ensure that releases meet acceptance criteria and quality standards, coordinating both manual and automated testing efforts.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain test plans and test cases aligned with acceptance criteria
- Own sign-off on release readiness and quality gates
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts across the team
- Collaborate with Developers to scope testing coverage and identify edge cases
- Communicate release readiness status to the Project Manager
- Facilitate User Acceptance Testing (UAT) with stakeholders when required
- Track defects and work with the team to prioritize and resolve them

### Goals
- Ensure every release meets defined quality and acceptance standards
- Reduce the number of defects reaching production
- Maintain comprehensive test coverage across critical user flows

### Typical Communication
- Test plan reviews with Developers and Product Managers
- Release readiness reports shared with the Project Manager
- UAT coordination with stakeholders and business owners
